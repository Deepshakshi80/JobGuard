About JobGuard

Making Job Searching
Safer with Machine Learning

JobGuard is a machine-learning-based project designed to help job seekers identify potentially fraudulent job postings and make more informed career decisions.

What is JobGuard?

Online job platforms make it easier to find employment opportunities, but fraudulent job advertisements can mislead applicants and expose them to financial loss or misuse of personal data.

JobGuard uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) and machine learning techniques to analyse the text of a job posting and predict whether it may be legitimate or potentially fraudulent.

Users can enter information from a job advertisement and receive a prediction to help them decide whether further verification is needed.

Our Main Objectives

  • ✓ Help job seekers recognise potentially fake job advertisements.
  • ✓ Apply machine learning to real-world employment-related problems.
  • ✓ Encourage users to verify job opportunities before sharing personal information.
  • ✓ Support safer and more informed employment decisions.

Warning Signs of Fake Jobs

Learn to identify common patterns that may indicate a fraudulent job advertisement.

  • Unusually high salary for very little work.
  • Requests for payment to secure a job.
  • Immediate selection without a proper process.
  • Unprofessional communication or suspicious links.
  • Requests for sensitive information too early.

Red Flags You Should Never Ignore

⚠️ Be Careful Before You Apply

✖The recruiter asks you to pay registration, training or placement fees.
✖You receive an offer without a credible interview or selection process.
✖The salary seems unrealistic compared with the responsibilities.
✖The recruiter pressures you to act immediately or keep the offer secret.
✖The company name, website or contact details cannot be independently verified.
✖The recruiter requests OTPs, passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Smart Job Search Tips

✓ Verify Before Applying

Search for the vacancy on the employer's official careers page and confirm that the recruiter is associated with the company.

✓ Never Pay for a Job Offer

Treat requests for advance payments as serious warning signs. Verify any claimed fees directly with the official organisation.

✓ Report Suspected Fraud

Preserve relevant messages, emails and transaction details. In India, report suspected cybercrime through the official government portal.

Important Information

JobGuard is an informational and machine-learning project intended to support job-posting assessment. Its prediction may be incorrect because of limitations in the training data and model. A legitimate-looking advertisement can still be fraudulent, and a suspicious prediction does not establish that an employer is a scammer. Verify job offers independently and use official channels to report suspected fraud.
